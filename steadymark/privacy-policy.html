What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not require registration and does not create an account. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or any other contact details to use it.

Specifically, the Application processes the following information:

  • Health data: the blood pressure readings you enter (systolic and diastolic pressure, pulse, the date and time of each reading, and any note you add), the start date of your 7-day measurement program, and the goal you choose during onboarding. This data is stored only in a database on your device. It is not sent to the Service Provider's servers or to any third party, and the Service Provider cannot see it.
  • Reports you export: PDF reports and CSV files are created on your device. They leave your device only when you choose to share or save them through your device's share sheet, and the app or person you share them with handles them under its own terms.
  • Usage data: Google Analytics for Firebase receives events about how the Application is used, such as onboarding steps, the goal category you choose (doctor visit, medication change, or keeping track), screens such as the report preview and the Premium screen, and purchase events (the plan and whether a free trial was used). These events never include your blood pressure readings, pulse, or notes.
  • Purchase data: if you buy Premium, the purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID it generates, so that the Application can verify and restore your Premium access. The Service Provider never receives your payment card details.
  • Advertising data: the free version of the Application shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. Premium users do not see ads, and the ad service is not started for them. To serve and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google AdMob receives information such as your device model, operating system, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), app version, ad interactions such as views and taps, and an app-scoped identifier (the identifier for vendors on iOS, or the app set ID on Android). The Application requests non-personalized ads only. It does not ask for permission to track you on iOS, it does not access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA), and it does not collect the Android advertising ID. Your blood pressure readings, pulse, notes, and goal are never sent to Google AdMob or used for advertising.

The Service Provider does not use your health data for advertising or marketing, does not show personalized ads, and does not sell your information.
