Where are my readings stored?

Only on your phone. SteadyMark has no account and never uploads your readings, so deleting the app deletes them. To keep a copy, export a PDF report or CSV file from the Doctor report screen (open History, then tap Doctor report).


How do I delete a reading?

Open the reading and tap the trash icon at the top right.


How do I delete all my data?

On the Today screen, tap the gear icon to open Settings, then tap Delete all data. This permanently deletes your readings, changes, and other app data on this device, and the app returns to the first-run setup. It can't be undone, so export a copy first if you want to keep one. Your Premium purchase isn't affected, and this does not cancel your subscription.


How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy Premium, tap the gear icon on the Today screen to open Settings, then tap Restore purchases.


How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.


Is SteadyMark medical advice?

No. SteadyMark helps you record readings and share them with your doctor. Talk to your doctor about your readings and before changing any medication. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
